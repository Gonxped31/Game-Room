Do, in this order:
1. Publish `database.rules.json` (it adds `nighthighway/options/desert`).
2. Deploy the app.
3. Race in the desert on a real phone.

Why the reversed order: CLAUDE.md says ship the client before the rules, but this change adds a field. The new client writes `options.desert` in every game-state write, and the old rules refuse unknown fields (`$other: false`), so the new app would break under the old rules. The new rules accept the old client because the field is optional.
